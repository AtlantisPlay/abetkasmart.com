Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about using MakeAMovie AI to create stunning videos.

Getting Started

MakeAMovie AI is an innovative app that transforms your text descriptions into AI-generated video content. Simply describe the scene you want to create, and our AI technology will generate professional-quality video clips that match your vision.

Getting started is easy:

  • Download the app on your preferred platform (macOS, iOS, or Android)
  • Sign in with your Apple or Google account
  • Create a new project and start adding shots
  • Write descriptions for your shots and generate with AI

MakeAMovie AI is available on:

  • macOS - For Mac computers
  • iOS - For iPhone and iPad
  • Android - For Android phones and tablets

Creating Projects

To create a new project, tap the "+" button or "New Project" option from the main screen. Give your project a name and optionally add a description. Your project will be saved automatically as you work.

Inside your project, tap "Add Shot" to create a new shot. Each shot represents one video clip in your movie. You can add as many shots as you need to tell your story.

Projects are saved locally on your device. On macOS, you can choose your preferred storage location. On iOS and Android, projects are stored in the app's secure storage area. Your projects remain private and are not uploaded to any server.

Yes! You can drag and drop shots to reorder them within your project. Simply tap and hold a shot, then drag it to your desired position.

AI Generation & Tokens

Our AI analyzes your text description and generates a video clip that matches what you've written. The more detailed and specific your description, the better the results. You can describe scenes, actions, camera movements, lighting, and mood.

Tokens are the currency used to generate AI videos. Each video generation consumes tokens based on the duration and complexity of the output. You receive some free tokens when you sign up, and you can purchase additional token packs as needed.

Token usage varies based on the video duration and quality settings. Shorter clips use fewer tokens, while longer or higher-quality clips use more. The app will show you the estimated token cost before you generate.

Yes! If a generated shot doesn't match your vision, you can regenerate it with the same or modified description. Each regeneration uses tokens, so you may want to refine your description first for better results.

You can purchase token packs directly in the app. Go to your account settings and select "Buy Tokens" to see available packages. Payments are processed securely through the App Store or Google Play.

Export & Sharing

Once you're happy with your project, tap the "Export" button to compile all your shots into a single video file. You can choose the output quality and format, then save the video to your device or share it directly.

Exported videos are saved in MP4 format, which is widely compatible with most devices, social media platforms, and video editing software.

Absolutely! After exporting your video, you can share it directly to social media platforms, messaging apps, or save it to your photo library for later sharing.

Account & Billing

You can sign in using your existing Apple ID or Google account. This makes it easy to get started without creating a new username and password. Your account is automatically created when you first sign in.

Yes! All purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment information directly. These platforms use industry-standard encryption and security measures.

Refund requests for in-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google, depending on your platform. Please contact their support for refund inquiries. Note that tokens that have already been used cannot be refunded.

You can request account deletion from within the app's settings. Go to Account Settings and select "Delete Account". This will remove your account data from our servers. Note that projects stored locally on your device will not be affected.
