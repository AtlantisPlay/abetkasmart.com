Princess Birthday Games

Princess Lily and her little rainbow unicorn get ready for a birthday celebration with friends, treats, songs, and independent learning games for ages 3-5.

Children count, match, trace, decorate, and create as part of Lily's birthday story. No reading or voiceover is needed; the scene and characters show what to do.

Inside the game

  • Count candles, gifts, and balloons from 1 to 5
  • Trace rainbows, numbers, and shapes with a fingertip
  • Match colors, shapes, gifts, and sweet treats
  • Sort, compare, and solve simple puzzles
  • Decorate cupcakes, cakes, and a princess dress
  • Follow Lily's story across 9 animated chapters

Why our games feel different

♡

Learning begins inside the story.

A child can match shapes anywhere. But matching gifts for a friend, counting candles for a cake, or tracing a path through the snow feels different.

The activity has a reason. The story gives it meaning.

✦

Designed around early-childhood learning principles:

  • Guided play
  • Montessori-inspired independence
  • Visual logic and sorting
  • Early math
  • Attention and memory
  • Planning and self-control

No scores. No pressure. No reward loops. Children keep going because they care what happens next.

Our approach

A gentle rhythm for little players.

♡

Each game moves in a gentle rhythm: story moment, learning activity, story moment.

Children play through one clear emotional journey, with time to understand, try, watch, and return to the story.

Like a favorite picture book, each game has a beginning and a gentle ending. Children finish it on their own, so playtime ends with a smile instead of being cut short, and the next day they ask to play it again.
