Princess Birthday Games

Help Princess Lily and her rainbow unicorn prepare for a special birthday through animated story chapters and independent learning games for ages 3-5.

No reading or voiceover is needed. The game guides children through visual context, original music, and beautiful songs. No scores, no pressure, and no stressful reward loops.

What your child will do

  • Count candles, gifts, and balloons from 1 to 5
  • Trace rainbows, numbers, and shapes with a fingertip
  • Match colors, shapes, gifts, and sweet treats
  • Sort, compare, and solve simple puzzles
  • Decorate cupcakes, cakes, and party dresses
  • Follow Lily's story across 9 animated chapters

How learning works

First skills, gently practiced.

✦

Short, visual activities help little hands practice matching, sorting, finding, tracing, counting, and creating.

Each activity is small enough for young children, clear enough for independent play, and connected to the story so the child understands why it matters.

Count Count candles for a birthday cake.
Trace Trace a rainbow path that helps the story move forward.
Match Match cups, gifts, shapes, and story objects.
Follow Guide a little friend along a path.
Sort Sort berries, treats, stockings, colors, and early categories.
Decorate Decorate cakes, trees, letters, gifts, and story moments.

Little hands practice. Little minds connect. The story gives each activity meaning.

Our approach

A gentle rhythm for little players.

♡

Each game moves in a gentle rhythm: a short story moment, a simple activity, then the next story moment.

Children do not move through random tasks. Each activity continues one emotional journey, giving young players time to understand, play, watch, and return to the story.
